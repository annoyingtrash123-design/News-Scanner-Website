# Daily Scanner Methodology Handover

Reference document supplied by the project owner. It describes the
three-tier daily scan (government, company announcements, competitor media),
the honesty and coverage rules, the reporting format and the mandatory
"Argus angle". Kept verbatim below with one exception: the KEYWORD UNIVERSE
section repeated the same lists as asia-critical-minerals-daily-scan.md
(BATTERY MINERALS through CORPORATE EVENTS, with "DEFENCE" and "EXPORT
CONTROLS" as the section names), so it is replaced by a pointer to that file.
Bullet characters were joined onto their lines. The text as received ends partway through
the Argus angle examples (after the export controls example), so the original
may have continued past that point.

```text

CORE PRINCIPLE
====================================================================
Accuracy > completeness > speed
User instruction (highest priority):
NEVER LIE.
BE HONEST ABOUT FAILURES, SHORTCOMINGS, MISSED SOURCES, LIMITATIONS

• a source was checked if it wasn't
• a company was checked if it wasn't
• 19/19 coverage unless all 19 were checked
• 37/37 coverage unless all 37 were checked
• full scan coverage unless actually achieved
Coverage counts must reflect ACTUAL coverage.
If a source was not checked:
Coverage incomplete.
List missed sources.

PERMANENT USER RULE
====================================================================
For all Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 scans:
Never report full coverage unless actually achieved.
Coverage counts must reflect sources genuinely checked.
Explicitly disclose missed sources.
Explicitly disclose limitations.
Explicitly disclose failures.
Explicitly disclose uncertainty.
Be honest about shortcomings and scan completeness.
This is the highest-priority operational rule.
====================================================================
ARGUS ANGLE (MANDATORY)
====================================================================
Every material story must end with:
ARGUS ANGLE:
This is mandatory for Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3.
Purpose:
Explain WHY Argus should care.
Do not simply repeat the announcement.
Focus on:
• commodity market impact
• supply chain implications
• pricing implications
• trade implications
• financing implications
• approvals significance
• competitive significance
• follow-up reporting opportunities
====================================================================
ARGUS ANGLE EXAMPLES
====================================================================
Government funding story
ARGUS ANGLE:
Government support may accelerate project development timelines and
increase future supply of the commodity.
Follow up:
• timing of drawdown
• production start date
• impact on competing projects
--------------------------------------------------
Mine approval
ARGUS ANGLE:
Approval materially de-risks the project and brings it closer to
construction and first production.
Follow up:
• financing status
• construction timing
• expected output
• offtake negotiations
--------------------------------------------------
Battery plant
ARGUS ANGLE:
The facility may increase domestic demand for lithium chemicals,
graphite anodes or cathode materials.
Follow up:
• feedstock source
• capacity
• commissioning date
• government incentives
--------------------------------------------------
Rare earth separation plant
ARGUS ANGLE:
Potentially reduces reliance on Chinese refining and creates new
non-Chinese processing capacity.
Follow up:
• separation capacity
• feedstock source
• customer contracts
• magnet market implications
--------------------------------------------------
Export controls
ARGUS ANGLE:
May tighten availability of material outside China and influence
prices, trade flows and stockpiling activity.
Follow up:
• licensing requirements
• affected products
• response from downstream consumers
```
